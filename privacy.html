Kinnest Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Kinnest helps family caregivers find unclaimed money that a parent or relative may be owed, and understand how to claim it. This policy explains what information the Kinnest app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

WhatWhy
Your email address and passwordTo create and sign in to your account. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
The name, optional birth year, and the cities, states, and optional ZIP codes lived in of the family member you set up a profile forTo search official state unclaimed-property records for that person.
Which claim documents you mark as readyTo keep your documents checklist in sync.

Information created when you use Kinnest

  • Matched records. When Kinnest scans, it compares the profile's name and cities or ZIP codes against public unclaimed-property data published by the California State Controller's Office, and saves any matches (for example amount, property type, and holder name) to your account.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and renewal history, and basic device information (such as device model, OS version, and app version) so we can tell whether you have Premium. RevenueCat does not receive your email address or any profile information.
  • Reminders. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Their content is not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never ask for, collect, or handle a Social Security number, bank details, or the claimed money itself. Claims are filed directly with the state on its own official website.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting (privacy policy).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (privacy policy).
  • Apple — payments through the App Store (privacy policy).

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account information and profiles for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated profiles, matches, and checklist data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. Deletion is permanent. Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are retained under their own policies; cancel an active subscription in your Apple ID settings, since deleting your Kinnest account does not cancel it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to each profile is restricted at the database level to the account that created it.

Children

Kinnest is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your information. You can edit profile locations in Settings, delete your account in the app, or contact us for any other request.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and notify you in the app for significant changes.
