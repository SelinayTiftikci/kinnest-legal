How does Kinnest find money?

When a bank, insurer, or company can't reach the owner of an account, check, or payout, the money is turned over to the state. Kinnest compares the name and past cities of the family member you set up against official state unclaimed property records and shows you any matches.

Which states are covered?

Kinnest scans California's records automatically. For Texas and New York, the app links you to each state's official search site so you can look up a name yourself.

Does it cost anything to claim the money?

No. Claims are free and are filed directly with the state on its own official website. Kinnest never takes a percentage of what you recover and never handles the money itself.

Can I claim on behalf of my parent?

Often, yes, with the right documents. Depending on the situation that may be a Power of Attorney, or estate documents if the owner has passed away. The Documents checklist in the app explains the common cases. It's informational only and not legal advice, so check the state's requirements for your situation.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Kinnest. You can also get there from Settings → View plans → Manage or Cancel Subscription inside Kinnest.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Premium back?

Open Settings → Upgrade (or View plans) in Kinnest and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I delete my account?

In Kinnest, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all profiles, matches, and checklists. It doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that separately if you have one.
